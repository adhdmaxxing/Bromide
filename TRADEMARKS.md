# The Bromide name and logo

Bromide's source code is free software under the GNU GPL v3: you may use, study,
change and share it, including in your own apps, under that license.

The GPL covers the code, not the name. As permitted by section 7(e) of the GPLv3,
no rights are granted to use the name "Bromide" or the Bromide logo and icon
(the glowing "bromide" wordmark and the "b" launcher icon, in `tools/banner/`,
`tools/icon/`, `docs/` and `app/src/main/res/`).

If you publish a modified version, or a build of your own, please:

- give it a different name and icon, so nobody mistakes it for Bromide;
- use a different application ID than `app.bromide.film`;
- feel free to say it is "based on Bromide", with a link to this repository.

Building Bromide for your own use, and sharing patches with this project, need
no permission at all.
